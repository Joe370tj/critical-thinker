Information we collect

SkillBridge AI may collect the name, email address, account role, service area, profile details, skills, job descriptions, applications, messages, reviews, and reports that users submit. The service also processes basic technical information needed to keep accounts secure and the service running.

How information is used

Information is used to create accounts, connect clients and workers, display relevant jobs and profiles, manage applications, support job-related messages, calculate explainable recommendations, handle reports, and protect the platform.

Visibility and sharing

Some profile details and job posts are visible to other platform users. Job conversations and private account records should only be accessible to authorised participants and administrators when needed for platform operations or complaint handling. Do not post sensitive personal information in public profiles or job descriptions.

Storage and security

Passwords are stored as hashes. No online service can guarantee absolute security. The operator should configure HTTPS, restrict administrator access, keep software updated, and make protected backups before accepting real users.

Retention and requests

The operator should define how long records are kept and provide a process for users to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained to resolve disputes or meet legal duties.

Children

The service is not designed for children to arrange jobs independently. The operator should set and enforce an appropriate minimum age and safeguarding process before launch.
